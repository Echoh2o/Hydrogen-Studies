# Scientific Reviewer — brief

Draft for Josh's search (PLAN.md §4.1). Edit freely before sending; budget and terms are Josh's to set.

## The role in one paragraph

Hydrogen Studies (hydrogenstudies.com) is a searchable database of 2,000+ published studies on molecular hydrogen, with plain-English summaries and evidence-graded topic pages. The site is funded by Echo Technologies LLC, which makes hydrogen water products, and says so on every page. We need one credentialed scientific reviewer — a methodologist — to check our summaries and topic pages for accuracy, sign off on them by name, and later author short living systematic reviews. Independence matters more than enthusiasm: we want someone who will tell us when a claim is wrong, including claims that would help our sponsor.

## Who we're looking for

**Required**
- PhD, MD or PharmD in a biomedical, nutrition, exercise-science or public-health field.
- Hands-on experience with **systematic reviews or meta-analyses** (author on at least one published SR/MA). This is the hard requirement — Phase 6 depends on it.
- Comfortable judging study design: RCT vs. observational vs. animal vs. in vitro, risk of bias, GRADE certainty.
- Willing to be named publicly on the site with credentials, photo and review dates.

**Nice to have**
- Published on molecular hydrogen, oxidative stress, exercise physiology or metabolic outcomes.
- Experience with clinical-trial registries and reporting standards (CONSORT, PRISMA).
- Plain-language writing for a lay audience.

**Disqualifying**
- Current paid relationship with a hydrogen product company other than this engagement (disclose any past ones).
- Unwilling to have their reviews or corrections published as written.

## What the work is

| Work | Volume | Cadence |
|---|---|---|
| Review topic (hub) pages — direct answer, evidence grade, sources, FAQ | 40+ pages (10 with written intros today), ~300–800 words each | 5 per week to start, then as updated |
| Spot-check AI-assisted data extraction (dose, concentration, duration, sample size, funding) against the papers | random 5% sample, ~25–50 rows per batch | per batch (next batch ready now) |
| Review study summaries and headlines on human trials | ~350 human studies; new ones as added | ~10–20 per week |
| Approve or correct reader-reported errors | low volume | as they arrive |
| **Phase 2:** author living reviews (pooled estimate, forest plot, GRADE) for outcomes with ≥5 RCTs — likely exercise recovery, metabolic markers, oxidative-stress biomarkers | 1 to start, ~3 total | quarterly updates |
| Optional: monthly research digest; occasional journal-club video | — | monthly |

Rough load to start: **4–6 hours per week**. Our team (Josh + AI tooling) does drafting, data entry and publishing; the reviewer checks and decides.

## Independence terms (non-negotiable on our side)

- The reviewer can reject, correct or downgrade any claim. We publish the correction, not a negotiated version.
- The reviewer never reviews or endorses Echo products, product pages or sponsor content. Their name appears only on editorial content.
- Studies funded by Echo Technologies are flagged as such on the site, and the reviewer grades them the same as any other.
- Compensation is a flat retainer (or hourly), never tied to conclusions, traffic or sales.
- Disclosure on the site: "Reviewed by [Name, credentials]. [Name] is paid by Echo Technologies for scientific review and has no other financial relationship with the company." (Adjust to the actual relationship.)

## What appears on the site

- Reviewer page: name, credentials, photo, affiliation, relevant publications, ORCID, disclosure.
- On each reviewed page: "Reviewed by [Name], [credentials] on [date]" — and only then `reviewedBy` / `lastReviewed` structured data (CLAUDE.md: no reviewedBy without a named reviewer).
- Methodology page updated to describe the review process and corrections policy.

## Where to look

- Authors of SR/MAs on molecular hydrogen (e.g. search PubMed: `hydrogen[ti] AND (systematic review[pt] OR meta-analysis[pt])`) — they know the literature and the weaknesses.
- University exercise-science and nutrition departments; sports-medicine fellows.
- Cochrane and Campbell author networks; evidence-synthesis groups at academic medical centers.
- Freelance medical-review marketplaces (vetted MD/PhD reviewers) — fastest, but filter hard for SR/MA experience.
- Molecular Hydrogen Institute contacts — useful for referrals; check for product conflicts.

## Screening questions

1. Which systematic reviews or meta-analyses have you authored, and what was your role?
2. Here is one of our topic pages [send /explore-by-condition/type-2-diabetes]. What would you change?
3. A small industry-funded RCT shows a large effect; two larger independent trials show none. How should a consumer page summarize that?
4. Any current or past financial relationships with supplement, water or device companies?
5. Are you comfortable being named publicly, and with your corrections being published?
6. What weekly availability and rate are you looking for?

## Trial task (paid)

Review 3 hub pages + 20 extracted study rows. Return tracked corrections and an evidence grade per page. ~3 hours. Use it to judge accuracy, candor and turnaround before signing a retainer.
