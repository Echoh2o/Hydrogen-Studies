HydrogenStudies.com Redesign & Rebuild Outline for Replit

⸻

1. GOALS
	•	Create an intuitive, modern, and credible design that matches the scientific authority and accessibility of the site.
	•	Improve navigation and discoverability of hydrogen health studies.
	•	Integrate AI chatbot for guided discovery.
	•	Add editorial content areas (blog, articles).
	•	Support SEO, fast load times, mobile optimization.

⸻

2. TECH STACK / TOOLS
	•	Frontend: React (Next.js recommended)
	•	Backend/API: Node.js or Python (for API endpoints and DB interaction)
	•	CMS: Consider Sanity, Contentful, or a lightweight MDX blog system
	•	Search: Algolia or ElasticSearch for intelligent, faceted search
	•	AI: Existing chatbot embedded via iframe or custom component
	•	Hosting: Vercel or Replit’s deployment environment

⸻

3. SITE STRUCTURE & PAGE TYPES

A. Homepage
	•	Hero section with value proposition: “Understand the Science of Hydrogen for Health”
	•	Search bar front and center
	•	Featured studies or categories (e.g., “Brain Health,” “Anti-Inflammatory”)
	•	Links to: Chatbot, Articles, Blog, About, Contact

B. Study Browser Page
	•	Filter sidebar:
	•	Category (Disease type, Body system)
	•	Study type (Animal, Human, In vitro)
	•	Publication year
	•	Outcome (Positive/Neutral/Negative)
	•	Sort by: Date, Relevance, Study strength
	•	Card grid of studies:
	•	Title, short abstract, key tags, link to full page

C. Individual Study Page
	•	Study metadata (title, authors, journal, date)
	•	Full abstract
	•	Plain-language summary
	•	Related tags and links to similar studies
	•	AI chatbot quick summary suggestion: “Explain this to me like I’m a teenager”

D. Articles Page (Editorial Content)
	•	List view of editorial articles analyzing trends, compiling groups of studies (e.g., “Top 5 Hydrogen Studies on Gut Health”)
	•	Tag-based navigation
	•	Author profiles

E. Blog Page
	•	Posts from the EchoWater team or guest writers
	•	Date-sorted list, with optional categories (Company News, Product Applications, etc.)

F. About Us Page
	•	Mission statement
	•	Team bios
	•	Link to EchoWater and HydrogenStudies partnership purpose

G. Contact Page
	•	Secure form submission (with spam protection)
	•	Optional chatbot assistant for routing inquiry (Sales, Research Question, Feedback)

H. 404 / Empty Search Result Page
	•	Smart suggestions: top searches, links to chatbot, popular studies

⸻

4. NAVIGATION & UX
	•	Persistent header navigation with dropdowns:
	•	Studies
	•	Articles
	•	Blog
	•	About
	•	Contact
	•	Mobile hamburger menu
	•	Footer with social links, newsletter signup, sitemap

⸻

5. SEARCH & FILTERS
	•	Algolia or ElasticSearch-based full-text search
	•	Instant suggestions
	•	Filters for:
	•	Topic (Brain, Inflammation, Metabolism, etc.)
	•	Study type (Animal/Human/In vitro)
	•	Tags
	•	Date
	•	Synonym support and typo tolerance

⸻

6. AI CHATBOT INTEGRATION
	•	Persistent or floating button UI
	•	Conversation starter suggestions:
	•	“Find me studies about hydrogen and anxiety”
	•	“Summarize the newest findings in layman’s terms”
	•	Hand-off from study pages to chatbot for deeper interaction

⸻

7. ADDITIONAL FEATURES
	•	Dark mode toggle
	•	SEO metadata per page
	•	Social sharing buttons
	•	Structured data (Schema.org) for all studies and blog posts
	•	Email subscription modal or sidebar for new studies/articles
	•	Admin backend to upload and tag studies (or Replit CMS workflow)
