1. Structure by Use Case (Not Just Publication Type)

Top-Level Navigation:
	•	Explore by Benefit (pain relief, brain health, skin, energy, etc.)
	•	Explore by Condition (autoimmune, Alzheimer’s, IBS, etc.)
	•	Explore by System/Organ (brain, skin, gut, cardiovascular, etc.)
	•	Explore by Demographic (men, women, children, athletes, elderly)
	•	See All Studies (with search, filters, and sort options)

⸻

2. Apply Smart Tagging

Tags for Filtering:
	•	Organ/System: Brain, Gut, Skin, Heart, Lungs, etc.
	•	Disease/Condition: Diabetes, Stroke, Psoriasis, Anxiety, etc.
	•	Mechanism: Antioxidant, Anti-inflammatory, Mitochondrial, Apoptosis-regulation
	•	Study Type: Human Clinical, Animal Model, In Vitro, Review
	•	Demographic: Male/Female, Age group, Athletes
	•	Delivery Method: Inhalation, Drinking water, Bathing, IV, Tablet
	•	Duration: Acute, Chronic, Long-Term Follow-up
	•	Result Type: Positive, Mixed, Inconclusive

These tags can be combined for dynamic search filtering (like Amazon’s faceted filters).

⸻

3. Add Consumer-Friendly Summaries to Each Study

For every study, display:
	•	Quick Summary in Plain English:
“In this 2021 human study, drinking hydrogen water improved memory and reduced oxidative stress in adults with early-stage Alzheimer’s.”
	•	Key Details in Icons/Badges:
	•	Human Study
	•	30 participants
	•	12 weeks
	•	Hydrogen Water
	•	Improved sleep & reduced fatigue
	•	Key Outcome Icons:
	•	Up arrow = improvement
	•	Checkmark = statistically significant
	•	Caution sign = inconclusive
	•	“Why this matters” box:
One paragraph on how this applies to everyday life

⸻

4. Add Visuals
	•	Infographics or images summarizing mechanisms and results
	•	Simple anatomical illustrations with highlighted organ systems
	•	Color-coded outcome tags (Green = beneficial, Yellow = uncertain, Red = no effect)

⸻

5. Personal Relevance Tool

A simple tool that lets users select:
	•	Their age, gender
	•	Any health goals or conditions
	•	Preferred delivery method (drink vs. inhale)

And it suggests relevant studies (or even products that relate to those studies).

⸻

6. Optional: Ratings + Clinical Translation

If possible:
	•	Add a scientific confidence score (e.g., from 1–5 based on study quality, N size, etc.)
	•	Show whether this is ready for clinical use, early-stage, or speculative

⸻

7. Technical/Professional Corner

Still provide:
	•	Abstracts
	•	Full citation
	•	DOI links
	•	PubMed links
	•	Filters by author, journal, year, etc.

⸻

Bonus: Content Clusters

Create curated “topic pages” like:
	•	Hydrogen for Brain Health
	•	Top 10 Studies on Hydrogen and Inflammation
	•	Hydrogen & Women’s Health
	•	Is Molecular Hydrogen Safe for Kids?

These act like educational landing pages or guides, improving SEO and time-on-site.
